# Primary Source References

The 12 primary sources cited across this bundle's concepts, each a first-class `type: Reference` document so citations resolve inside the bundle (PROFILE.md §3.2).

* [Nāsadīya Sūkta -- Ṛg Veda 10.129](rigveda-nasadiya.md) - Rig Veda 10.129; the creation hymn that ends in cosmological agnosticism.
* [Puruṣa Sūkta -- Ṛg Veda 10.90](rigveda-purusha-sukta.md) - Rig Veda 10.90; the cosmic-person hymn of creation through self-offering.
* [Hiraṇyagarbha Sūkta -- Ṛg Veda 10.121](rigveda-hiranyagarbha-sukta.md) - Rig Veda 10.121; the golden-embryo cosmogony hymn (kasmai devaya havisha vidhema).
* [Bhāgavata Purāṇa](bhagavata-purana.md) - Vaishnava maha-purana; Krishna's life and the dashavatara frame, a primary source for lila and creation accounts.
* [Viṣṇu Purāṇa](vishnu-purana.md) - Maha-purana central to Vaishnava cosmology; standard source for kalpa structure and srshti-sthiti-samhara.
* [Brahma Purāṇa](brahma-purana.md) - One of the eighteen maha-puranas; creation narratives and tirtha accounts.
* [Mārkaṇḍeya Purāṇa](markandeya-purana.md) - Early maha-purana; frame narratives, cosmology, and the Devi-Mahatmya within it.
* [Matsya Purāṇa](matsya-purana.md) - Maha-purana of the fish avatara; the flood narrative and kalpa-manvantara reckonings.
* [Sāṃkhya Kārikā](samkhya-karika.md) - Ishvarakrishna's root text of classical Samkhya; source of the tattva scaffolding the Puranas inherit.
* [Manu-Smṛti](manusmriti.md) - The dharmashastra of Manu; its cosmogony chapter grounds the time-cycle and creation vocabulary.
* [Brahma Sūtras (Vedānta Sūtras)](brahmasutras.md) - Badarayana's aphoristic systematization of Upanishadic teaching; the root text of Vedanta.
* [Devī Māhātmya (Durgā Saptaśatī)](devi-mahatmya.md) - Markandeya Purana chapters 81-93; the Goddess as ultimate power, root text of Shakta theology.

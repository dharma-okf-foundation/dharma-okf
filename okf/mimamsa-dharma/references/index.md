# Primary Source References

The 7 primary sources cited across this bundle's concepts, each a first-class `type: Reference` document so citations resolve inside the bundle (PROFILE.md §3.2).

* [Artha-saṃgraha (Laugākṣi Bhāskara)](artha-samgraha.md) - Laugākṣi Bhāskara''''s concise primer of Pūrva-Mīmāṃsā, the standard short introduction to injunction, prohibition, arthavāda, mantra, and the means of ritual application.
* [Bṛhatī (Prabhākara Miśra)](brhati.md) - Prabhākara Miśra''''s commentary on the Śabara-Bhāṣya, the founding text of the Prābhākara school, developing the injunction as niyoga and kārya, the anvitābhidhāna theory of sentence meaning, and the self as co-revealed knower.
* [Mīmāṃsā Sūtra (Jaimini)](mimamsa-sutra.md) - Jaimini''s Pūrva-Mīmāṃsā Sūtra, the root text of the school, opening with the definition of dharma as codanā-lakṣaṇa and setting out the rules for reading Vedic injunctions.
* [Śabara-Bhāṣya](sabara-bhashya.md) - Śabarasvāmin''s commentary on the Mīmāṃsā Sūtra, the earliest surviving Bhāṣya and the text through which Jaimini''s sūtras were classically read; the common ground later divided by Kumārila and Prabhākara.
* [Śāstra-dīpikā (Pārthasārathi Miśra)](sastra-dipika.md) - Pārthasārathi Miśra''''s systematic Bhāṭṭa manual, a standard later exposition of the whole Mīmāṃsā system used to present the Bhāṭṭa positions and their contrast with the Prābhākara.
* [Ślokavārttika (Kumārila Bhaṭṭa)](slokavarttika.md) - Kumārila Bhaṭṭa''''s verse commentary on the first pāda of the Śabara-Bhāṣya, the founding text of the Bhāṭṭa school, developing perception, inference, testimony, intrinsic validity, the eternal word, and the realist self.
* [Tantravārttika (Kumārila Bhaṭṭa)](tantravarttika.md) - Kumārila''''s commentary on the later pādas of the Śabara-Bhāṣya, extending the Bhāṭṭa analysis of injunction, apūrva, the subordination of arthavāda and mantra, and the rules of ritual application.

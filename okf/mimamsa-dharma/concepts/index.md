# Concepts

The 25 concept documents in this directory, in the order and groupings of the bundle index. Each is a `type: Concept` document carrying `not:` and `darshana:` (PROFILE.md §2.1, §2.2).

## Tier 1 -- The Injunction Engine (CRITICAL AI risk)

* [Dharma (Mīmāṃsā)](dharma.md) - In Pūrva-Mīmāṃsā, dharma is codanā-lakṣaṇa artha, the good knowable only through Vedic injunction; NOT religion, NOT ethics-by-reason, NOT the general sustaining-order, NOT autonomous duty.
* [Codanā](codana.md) - The Vedic injunction or impulsion, the impersonal imperative force (borne by the injunctive verb-forms) that both reveals dharma and prompts the agent to act; NOT a command from a commander, NOT advice, NOT mere motivation.
* [Vidhi](vidhi.md) - The injunction proper, the Vedic sentence-type that enjoins an as-yet-unaccomplished action; the operative core through which dharma is conveyed; NOT a commandment from a divine legislator, NOT a human statute, NOT a merely descriptive rule.
* [Niṣedha](nisedha.md) - The prohibition, the negative Vedic injunction that enjoins abstention from an action; a full injunction in its own right, NOT a mere absence of injunction, NOT a social taboo, NOT a human-enforced ban.
* [Arthavāda](arthavada.md) - The explanatory and eulogistic statements of the Veda that praise an enjoined act or blame a forbidden one; subordinate to injunction and read as one sentence with it, NOT independent assertions of fact, NOT literal doctrine, NOT mere decoration.

## Tier 2 -- Unseen Link and Authorless Authority (CRITICAL / HIGH)

* [Apūrva](apurva.md) - The unseen after-potency by which a completed, momentary rite produces its remote result; the Bhāṭṭa answer to how a sacrifice now yields heaven later; NOT merit-points, NOT a karmic bank balance, NOT divine reward.
* [Apauruṣeyatva](apauruseyatva.md) - The authorlessness of the Veda: the doctrine that the Veda has no author, human or divine, being an eternal, uncreated relation of word and meaning; NOT divine revelation, NOT scripture written by God, NOT prophecy.
* [Śabda-nityatā](sabda-nityata.md) - The doctrine that the word (śabda) and its meaning-bearing relation are eternal, manifested rather than created by each utterance; the metaphysical ground of the Veda''''s authorlessness; NOT the claim that a spoken noise lasts forever, NOT sound mysticism.
* [Autpattika](autpattika.md) - The doctrine that the relation of a word to its meaning is inherent and non-conventional, not established by anyone''''s agreement; the ground on which Vedic words mean reliably without an institutor; NOT arbitrary convention, NOT a social contract of language.
* [Svataḥ-prāmāṇya](svatah-pramanya.md) - The doctrine that a cognition''''s validity is intrinsic, arising with the cognition itself and holding unless a defeater appears; Mīmāṃsā''''s guarantee of the Veda''''s self-authority. School-scoped from its Mīmāṃsā source; NOT blind faith, NOT circular self-certification, NOT the claim that all cognitions are true.

## Tier 3 -- Action-Theory, the Two Schools (HIGH)

* [Bhāvanā](bhavana.md) - The productive urging toward a result that the Bhāṭṭa school takes to be the core meaning of a Vedic injunction, analysed by its object, instrument, and procedure; NOT manifestation in the wish-fulfilment sense, NOT emotion, NOT Buddhist meditative cultivation.
* [Niyoga](niyoga.md) - In the Prābhākara school, the binding directive or ought that is the very content of a Vedic injunction, obeyed as duty for its own sake; NOT a result-oriented incentive, NOT a request or wish, NOT a psychological compulsion.
* [Kārya (Prābhākara)](karya.md) - In Prābhākara Mīmāṃsā, the to-be-done, the noetic content of an injunction that the enjoined agent apprehends as needing to be accomplished, the correlate of niyoga; NOT a desired goal, NOT the physical action or product, NOT kārya-as-effect in the Nyāya sense.
* [Adhikāra](adhikara.md) - The eligibility or qualification that makes a person the addressee of a given injunction and the recipient of its fruit, fixed by desire-for-the-result and capacity; NOT authority over others, NOT a legal right, NOT caste status by itself.
* [Phala](phala.md) - The result an enjoined act is performed to yield, paradigmatically svarga, delivered through the act''''s unseen potency and specifying who has adhikāra; NOT a divine reward, NOT the immediate physical outcome, NOT liberation (mokṣa).

## Tier 4 -- Ritual Structure and Application (MEDIUM-HIGH)

* [Yāga / Yajña](yaga.md) - The Vedic sacrificial act, the karma of Mīmāṃsā, a bounded rule-governed performance efficacious through correct enactment and its unseen potency; NOT devotional worship, NOT magic, and a third distinct sense of karma from moral action or physical motion.
* [Aṅga-Pradhāna](anga-pradhana.md) - The for-the-sake-of relation between subsidiary acts (aṅga) and the principal rite (pradhāna), analysed as śeṣa-śeṣin (accessory-principal); the structure that orders a complex sacrifice; NOT a ranking of importance, NOT physical part and whole, NOT a hierarchy of persons.
* [Viniyoga](viniyoga.md) - The rule-governed application of a mantra, material, or subsidiary act to its rite, established by six graded means of evidence; NOT symbolic interpretation, NOT priestly improvisation, NOT free association.
* [Kratvartha and Puruṣārtha](kratvartha-purusartha.md) - The distinction between what is done for the sake of the sacrifice (kratvartha) and what is done for the sake of the agent (puruṣārtha); the criterion sorting subsidiary acts from fruit-bearing ones; NOT selfless versus selfish, NOT sacred versus secular, NOT means versus end in the ethical sense.
* [Nāmadheya](namadheya.md) - A Vedic word functioning as the mere name of a rite rather than as an injunction or a separate directive; a parsing category that blocks a naming-word from being misread as enjoining an extra act; NOT a magical true-name, NOT an arbitrary label, NOT a definition.

## Tier 5 -- Language and Realist Ontology (MEDIUM)

* [Abhihitānvaya](abhihitanvaya.md) - The Bhāṭṭa theory of sentence meaning: words first express their individual meanings, which are then connected into a sentence-sense by a further operation, so word-meaning is primary and sentence-meaning derived; NOT modern compositionality, NOT the claim that sentences are meaningless, NOT syntax.
* [Anvitābhidhāna](anvitabhidhana.md) - The Prābhākara theory of sentence meaning: words denote only as already connected with the other words'''' meanings, so sentence-sense is primary and isolated word-meaning an abstraction; NOT the denial of word-meaning, NOT modern holism as a general theory, NOT syntax-first grammar.
* [Mantra (Mīmāṃsā)](mantra.md) - In Mīmāṃsā, a Vedic formula whose ritual role is to express and bring to mind the elements of the act during performance, subordinate to injunction; NOT a magic spell, NOT a mystically powerful vibration, NOT a meaningless syllable.
* [Jāti (Mīmāṃsā)](jati.md) - The real universal, the eternal generic property inhering in each of its particulars, which grounds the fixed word-meaning relation; Mīmāṃsā realism about kinds; NOT a mental concept, NOT a class understood as its collection, NOT a nominal label.
* [Ātman (Mīmāṃsā)](atman.md) - In Mīmāṃsā, the real, plural, permanent self that performs enjoined acts and persists to enjoy their fruits in a later life, inferred because the ritual economy requires an enduring agent-enjoyer; NOT the Advaita non-dual Self, NOT a momentary bundle-self, NOT the body or mind.

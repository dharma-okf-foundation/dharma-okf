# Concepts

The 26 concept documents in this directory, in the order and groupings of the bundle index. Each is a `type: Concept` document carrying `not:` and `darshana:` (PROFILE.md §2.1, §2.2).

## Group 1 — Sāṃkhya Ontological Frame (6 concepts)

* [Puruṣa](purusha.md) - Pure inactive witness-consciousness, eternally distinct from Prakṛti — not soul, not self, not Ātman.
* [Prakṛti (Yoga Darśana)](prakriti-yoga.md) - The real primordial substrate evolving into mind and matter alike — not nature, not matter, not Māyā.
* [Guṇa-s](guna.md) - The three constituents of Prakṛti (sattva, rajas, tamas) — what Prakṛti is made of — not qualities, not energies.
* [Sattva](sattva.md) - The guṇa of clarity and luminosity in Prakṛti — not goodness, not purity as a moral trait.
* [Rajas](rajas.md) - The guṇa of motion and activity in Prakṛti — not passion, not energy, not aggression.
* [Tamas](tamas.md) - The guṇa of inertia and concealment in Prakṛti — not darkness as metaphor, not evil.

## Group 2 — Kleśa-s and Obstacles (6 concepts)

* [Kleśa](klesha.md) - The five structural afflictions that generate saṃsāric experience — not mere emotions, not moral failings.
* [Avidyā (Yoga Darśana)](avidya-yoga.md) - Active misidentification of the eternal witness with the body-mind, the root kleśa — not mere ignorance, not illusion.
* [Asmitā](asmita.md) - The misidentification of Puruṣa with Buddhi, the I-am-ness kleśa — not ego, not pride.
* [Rāga](raga.md) - The structural pull toward remembered pleasure, the third kleśa — not attachment, not love, not desire.
* [Dveṣa](dvesha.md) - The structural aversion built from remembered pain, the fourth kleśa — not hatred, not anger.
* [Abhiniveśa](abhinivesha.md) - The pre-conscious clinging to continued existence (the fifth kleśa) — not fear of death, not survival instinct.

## Group 3 — Citta Dynamics (5 concepts)

* [Citta-Vṛtti](chitta-vritti.md) - The modifications of citta that Yoga aims to still — not thoughts, not the mind, not mental chatter.
* [Vṛtti](vritti.md) - A single modification or wave of citta (five kinds) — not a thought, not a fluctuation only.
* [Saṃskāra](samskara.md) - The latent impression each experience deposits in citta, driving future vṛttis — not memory, not habit.
* [Vāsanā](vasana.md) - The accumulated directional tendency arising from saṃskāras — not desire, not a mere subliminal impression.
* [Nirodha](nirodha.md) - The cessation of citta's modifications that reveals Puruṣa — not suppression, not control, not emptiness.

## Group 4 — Absorption States (5 concepts)

* [Samprajñāta Samādhi](samprajnata-samadhi.md) - Cognitive absorption with an object, in four stages — not concentration, not trance.
* [Asamprajñāta Samādhi](asamprajnata-samadhi.md) - Objectless absorption in which all Prakṛtic cognition ceases and Puruṣa alone remains — not unconsciousness, not deep sleep.
* [Samāpatti](samapatti.md) - The cognitive coincidence in which citta takes the form of its object in samādhi — not union, not merger.
* [Pariṇāma](parinama.md) - Transformation in the satkāryavāda sense, latent becoming manifest — not change, not evolution, not progress.
* [Kaivalya](kaivalya.md) - Liberation as the permanent isolation of Puruṣa from Prakṛti — not mokṣa, not union, not oneness.

## Group 5 — Eight-Limb Deep Dives (4 concepts)

* [Pratyāhāra](pratyahara.md) - The fifth limb: the senses following citta inward rather than chasing objects — not sense-blocking, not detachment.
* [Dhāraṇā](dharana.md) - The sixth limb: binding citta to a single object — not generic meditation, not mere focus.
* [Viveka-Khyāti](viveka-khyati.md) - Sustained discriminative awareness of Puruṣa's difference from Prakṛti — not a flash of insight, not generic wisdom.
* [Īśvara (Yoga Darśana)](ishvara-yoga.md) - A special ever-unentangled Puruṣa, object of meditation and source of transmission — not a creator God, not Brahman, not Ātman.

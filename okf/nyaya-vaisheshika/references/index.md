# Primary Source References

The 10 primary sources cited across this bundle's concepts, each a first-class `type: Reference` document so citations resolve inside the bundle (PROFILE.md §3.2).

## Navya-Nyāya: named and deferred

* [Tattvacintāmaṇi (Gaṅgeśa)](tattvacintamani.md) - Gaṅgeśa's founding text of Navya-Nyāya ('new logic'), which rebuilt the theory of inference and cognition with a rigorous technical vocabulary; cited here as the marker for the Navya-Nyāya layer deferred to a future bundle.

## References (10 primary texts)

* [Nyāya Sūtra](nyaya-sutra.md) - The foundational sūtra text of the Nyāya school (Gautama / Akṣapāda), five books setting out the sixteen categories, the four pramāṇas, the theory of inference and debate, and the path to liberation through true knowledge.

## Primary Sources

* [Nyāya Bhāṣya (Vātsyāyana)](nyaya-bhashya.md) - Vātsyāyana's commentary on the Nyāya Sūtra, the earliest surviving Bhāṣya, which fixed the classical readings of perception, inference, testimony, the self, and the theory of error for the whole tradition.
* [Nyāyakusumāñjali (Udayana)](nyayakusumanjali.md) - Udayana's classic work of rational theism, marshalling inferential arguments for the existence of Īśvara as the intelligent efficient cause of the world; with his Ātmatattvaviveka and Kiraṇāvalī, the high point of classical Nyāya-Vaiśeṣika.
* [Nyāyamañjarī (Jayanta Bhaṭṭa)](nyayamanjari.md) - Jayanta Bhaṭṭa's independent, discursive treatise on Nyāya epistemology, prized for its lucid defence of the four pramāṇas and the reliabilist account of testimony.
* [Nyāyavārttika (Uddyotakara)](nyayavarttika.md) - Uddyotakara's sub-commentary defending Nyāya realism, especially the five-membered inference and the enduring self, against Buddhist logico-epistemology.
* [Padārthadharmasaṃgraha (Praśastapāda)](padarthadharmasamgraha.md) - Praśastapāda's systematic exposition of the Vaiśeṣika categories, so authoritative that its author is called the 'second founder'; the classic source for the twenty-four qualities and the dyad-triad composition.
* [Saptapadārthī (Śivāditya)](saptapadarthi.md) - Śivāditya's compact primer on the seven Vaiśeṣika categories, an accessible classical summary of the realist ontology.
* [Tarkasaṃgraha (Annambhaṭṭa)](tarkasamgraha.md) - Annambhaṭṭa's concise late primer of Nyāya-Vaiśeṣika, the standard classroom introduction to the categories, the pramāṇas, inference, and the fallacies.
* [Vaiśeṣika Sūtra](vaisheshika-sutra.md) - The foundational sūtra text of the Vaiśeṣika school (Kaṇāda), which sets out the categories of being, the nine substances, the qualities and motions, and the atomist cosmology.

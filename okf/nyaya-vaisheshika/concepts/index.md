# Concepts

The 27 concept documents in this directory, in the order and groupings of the bundle index. Each is a `type: Concept` document carrying `not:` and `darshana:` (PROFILE.md §2.1, §2.2).

## Tier 1 -- The Inference Engine (CRITICAL AI risk)

* [Anumāna](anumana.md) - Inference as a knowledge-yielding movement of cognition from a perceived mark to what it invariably pervades, grounded in vyāpti and displayed in five members; NOT the Aristotelian syllogism, NOT a purely formal deduction over propositions, NOT probable guessing.
* [Vyāpti](vyapti.md) - Invariable concomitance, the pervasion 'wherever the mark, there the pervaded' that licenses every inference; established by repeated observation, co-absence, and tarka; NOT mere correlation, NOT material implication true by definition, NOT statistical association.
* [Pañcāvayava](panchavayava.md) - The five members in which a demonstrative inference is stated to another (pratijñā, hetu, udāharaṇa, upanaya, nigamana); the public form of inference-for-others; NOT a redundant, longer version of the three-term syllogism, NOT rhetorical padding, NOT a proof in symbolic logic.
* [Hetu / Liṅga](hetu.md) - The probative reason or inferential mark: the feature whose presence signals the property to be established, valid only when it meets the required conditions (belonging to the subject, present in like cases, absent from all unlike cases); NOT merely ''the middle term'', NOT any correlated feature.
* [Tarka](tarka.md) - Suppositional or counterfactual reasoning that defends a pervasion by showing its denial leads to absurdity; an auxiliary that secures inference, not itself a pramāṇa; NOT 'logic' in the generic sense, NOT debate, NOT sophistry.
* [Hetvābhāsa](hetvabhasa.md) - The taxonomy of pseudo-reasons, marks that look probative but fail (classically fivefold: savyabhicāra, viruddha, satpratipakṣa, asiddha, bādhita); the Nyāya theory of inferential error; NOT the list of Western informal fallacies, NOT rhetorical foul play, NOT logical invalidity in the formal sense.

## Tier 2 -- The Pramāṇas from the Source School (HIGH; the v0.3 school-contrast tier)

* [Pramāṇa](pramana.md) - A means or instrument of valid knowing (pramā); Nyāya admits four (perception, inference, comparison, testimony) as realist instruments that reveal a mind-independent object; NOT 'proof' in the logician's sense, NOT subjective justification, NOT the Advaita treatment where the pramāṇas are finally sublated in non-dual knowing.
* [Pratyakṣa](pratyaksha.md) - Perception, the first pramāṇa: valid cognition arising from sense-object contact, in an indeterminate (nirvikalpaka) and a determinate (savikalpaka) phase; a realist contact-theory of knowing; NOT sense-data phenomenalism, NOT bare sensation, NOT the Advaita perception that is finally sublated.
* [Upamāna](upamana.md) - Comparison, the pramāṇa by which the tie between a name and an unfamiliar thing is known through told similarity (the classic gavaya case); an independent source of knowing, not reducible to inference or testimony; NOT analogy as a rhetorical device, NOT metaphor, NOT mere resemblance-noticing.
* [Śabda](shabda.md) - Verbal testimony, the pramāṇa yielding knowledge from the statement of a trustworthy speaker (āpta), its validity resting on the speaker's reliability; NOT scripture-inerrant-by-authorship, NOT mere hearsay, NOT the Śabda-Brahman of sound-metaphysics, NOT the Mīmāṃsā intrinsically-valid Veda.
* [Pramā / Prāmāṇya](pramanya.md) - Valid cognition (pramā) and the theory of how its validity is apprehended (prāmāṇya): Nyāya''s paratah-prāmāṇya, validity known extrinsically through corroboration and successful action, against Mīmāṃsā''s intrinsic svataḥ-prāmāṇya; NOT self-certifying intuition, NOT coherentism, NOT a definition of truth as usefulness.

## Tier 3 -- Debate and Reasoned Inquiry (HIGH)

* [Ṣoḍaśa-padārtha](shodasha-padartha.md) - The sixteen categories that organize Nyāya as a system, from pramāṇa and prameya through the debate terms to nigrahasthāna; the architecture of the school of reasoning; NOT Vaiśeṣika's seven ontological padārthas, NOT a metaphysics of what exists, NOT an arbitrary list.
* [Vāda](vada.md) - Honest, truth-seeking debate between parties who both argue only from valid pramāṇa and accept defeat when genuinely refuted; the highest of the three debate modes; NOT mere argument, NOT victory-seeking disputation (that is jalpa), NOT quarrel.
* [Jalpa](jalpa.md) - Victory-oriented disputation, in which winning is the goal and eristic devices (including chala, jāti, and nigrahasthāna) are permitted alongside valid reasoning; the middle debate mode; NOT honest truth-seeking debate (vāda), NOT outright lying, NOT idle quarrel.
* [Vitaṇḍā](vitanda.md) - Destructive, purely refutative debate that attacks and demolishes the opponent's thesis while advancing no counter-thesis of its own; the most eristic of the three modes; NOT skeptical philosophical method, NOT vāda, NOT constructive criticism.
* [Nigrahasthāna](nigrahasthana.md) - Points of defeat: the enumerated conditions under which a debater is judged to have lost, such as self-contradiction, shifting the thesis, incoherence, or falling silent; the procedural close of a formal debate; NOT the fallacies of inference (that is hetvābhāsa), NOT penalties for rudeness, NOT a scoring system for style.

## Tier 4 -- The Vaiśeṣika Categories: Realist Ontology (MEDIUM-HIGH)

* [Saptapadārtha](saptapadartha.md) - The seven categories of Vaiśeṣika ontology (dravya, guṇa, karma, sāmānya, viśeṣa, samavāya, abhāva); an exhaustive realist inventory of the kinds of the knowable; NOT Nyāya's sixteen epistemic-dialectical categories, NOT the Sāṃkhya twenty-five evolutes, NOT a list of substances only.
* [Dravya](dravya.md) - Substance, the first Vaiśeṣika category: the nine substrata (earth, water, fire, air, ether, time, space, self, mind) in which qualities and motions inhere; NOT Aristotelian primary substance, NOT matter or physical stuff, NOT a bare particular stripped of properties.
* [Guṇa](guna.md) - Quality, the second Vaiśeṣika category: the (twenty-four, per Praśastapāda) qualities that inhere in substances, colour, taste, number, contact, cognition, pleasure, effort, and the rest; NOT the Sāṃkhya triguṇa (sattva-rajas-tamas), NOT ''virtue'' or moral merit, NOT a strand or a Spinozan mode.
* [Karma](karma.md) - Motion, the third Vaiśeṣika category: physical movement of the five kinds (utkṣepaṇa throwing-up, apakṣepaṇa throwing-down, ākuñcana contraction, prasāraṇa expansion, gamana locomotion) that inheres momentarily in a substance and causes conjunction and disjunction; NOT the moral law of action-and-consequence, NOT ritual action, NOT "energy" or "force".
* [Sāmānya / Viśeṣa](samanya-vishesha.md) - Universal and ultimate particularity: sāmānya, the real repeatable universal that inheres in many individuals (cowness in cows), and viśeṣa, the ultimate differentiator individuating each eternal atom and self; realist universals, with viśeṣa the school''s namesake; NOT nominalist class-labels, NOT Platonic transcendent Forms, NOT mere resemblance.
* [Samavāya](samavaya.md) - Inherence, the sixth Vaiśeṣika category: the eternal, single, all-pervading relation that binds inseparables, whole and parts, substance and quality, motion and its locus, universal and individual, into one thing; NOT ordinary conjunction (saṃyoga), NOT set-membership, NOT physical attachment.

## Tier 5 -- Atomism and Realist Theism (MEDIUM)

* [Paramāṇu](paramanu.md) - The atom: the eternal, partless, imperceptible ultimate unit of the four elemental substances, qualitatively distinct and quality-bearing; NOT the Democritean atom, NOT the atom of modern physics, NOT a featureless mathematical point of matter.
* [Dvyaṇuka / Tryaṇuka](dvyanuka-tryanuka.md) - Dyad and triad: the composition mechanism by which two atoms form a dyad (dvyaṇuka) and three dyads form a triad (tryaṇuka), the smallest perceptible unit, building the gross world up from imperceptible atoms; NOT molecular chemistry, NOT a physical bonding theory, NOT physicalist emergence.
* [Ātman (Nyāya-Vaiśeṣika)](atman.md) - The self as an eternal substance and a real plurality: many distinct selves, each an eternal substratum in which cognition, pleasure, pain, desire, and effort inhere as adventitious qualities; NOT the single non-dual Ātman of Advaita, NOT the Abrahamic soul, NOT a stream or bundle of consciousness.
* [Īśvara (Nyāya-Vaiśeṣika)](ishvara.md) - God as the intelligent efficient cause of the ordered world, established by inference: the omniscient eternal self who fashions the cosmos from beginningless atoms and karma; the Nyāya rational theism (Udayana''s proofs); NOT the material cause of the world, NOT the Advaita Brahman appearing as Īśvara, NOT the Yoga Īśvara (a special untouched puruṣa who is not creator).
* [Anyathā-khyāti](anyatha-khyati.md) - The Nyāya realist theory of perceptual error: in illusion a real object is apprehended ''otherwise'', a really-existing silver, elsewhere and now remembered, is misperceived as located in the really-present shell, so both relata are real and only their relation is wrong; NOT Advaita''s anirvacanīya-khyāti / adhyāsa (superimposition of the indeterminable), NOT hallucination of an unreal object, NOT a coherence failure.

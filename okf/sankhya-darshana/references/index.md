# Primary Source References

The 7 primary sources cited across this bundle's concepts, each a first-class `type: Reference` document so citations resolve inside the bundle (PROFILE.md §3.2).

* [Bhagavad Gītā, Chapter 13 (Kṣetra-Kṣetrajña)](bhagavad-gita-ch13.md) - The Gita's chapter on the Field (kshetra, prakriti's domain) and the Knower of the Field (kshetrajna, purusha), a well-known cross-traditional parallel to Samkhya's purusha-prakriti dualism, cited here as a secondary, non-Samkhya-specific anchor illustrating the dualism's wider resonance in the corpus -- not treated as a Samkhya-school primary text.
* [Gauḍapāda-Bhāṣya (on the Sāṃkhya-Kārikā)](gaudapada-bhashya.md) - An early commentary on the Samkhya-Karika attributed to Gaudapada, providing verse-by-verse elaboration used to ground the interpretation of purusha's plurality and the guna apparatus in this bundle.
* [Sāṃkhya-Kārikā](samkhya-karika.md) - The foundational extant systematic text of classical Samkhya, composed by Isvarakrishna circa the 4th-5th century CE. Its 72 teaching stanzas state the satkaryavada argument (Karika 9), the 25-tattva structure with purusha's plurality, the triguna apparatus, and the viveka-khyati path to kaivalya. This bundle's primary textual anchor for purusha, prakriti, satkaryavada, parinamavada, vyakta-avyakta, guna-samkhya, sattva, rajas, viveka-khyati, kaivalya-samkhya, avidya-samkhya, bandha-samkhya, pramana-samkhya, anumana-samkhya, and samkhya-as-enumeration.
* [Sāṃkhya-Pravacana-Sūtra](samkhya-pravacana-sutra.md) - A sutra-format collection attributed to the Kapila tradition of Samkhya, extant as a later compilation. Verse 1.92 states directly that Ishvara is unproved, anchoring classical Samkhya's nirishvara doctrine. This bundle's secondary textual anchor for nirishvara, sesvara-samkhya, and kapila.
* [Sāṃkhya-Tattva-Kaumudī](samkhya-tattva-kaumudi.md) - Vachaspati Mishra's influential commentary on the Samkhya-Karika, a major secondary elaboration of classical Samkhya doctrine widely used to clarify Isvarakrishna's compact verses, including the causal arguments underlying satkaryavada and the structure of prakriti's avyakta phase.
* [Tattva-Samāsa](tattva-samasa.md) - A very short, aphoristic Samkhya text enumerating the tattvas in condensed form, sometimes considered an even earlier or parallel systematization to the Samkhya-Karika. Referenced here as supporting background for the samkhya-as-enumeration concept's discussion of the school's enumerative method.
* [Yuktidīpikā](yuktidipika.md) - An anonymous, philosophically rich early commentary on the Samkhya-Karika, valued for its detailed engagement with rival schools' objections to Samkhya's causal and epistemological positions, useful background for this bundle's satkaryavada and pramana-samkhya treatments.

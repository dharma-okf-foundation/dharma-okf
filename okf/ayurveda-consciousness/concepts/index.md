# Concepts

The 26 concept documents in this directory, in the order and groupings of the bundle index. Each is a `type: Concept` document carrying `not:` and `darshana:` (PROFILE.md §2.1, §2.2).

## Tier 1 -- The Constitution Engine (CRITICAL AI risk)

* [Doṣa](dosha.md) - One of the three functional bio-regulatory principles (vāta, pitta, kapha) whose dynamic equilibrium constitutes health and whose vitiation drives disease; NOT a bodily humor, NOT a body type, NOT a personality category.
* [Vāta](vata.md) - The kinetic doṣa, the principle of movement, transmission, and initiation, governing respiration, circulation, impulse conduction, and elimination; NOT wind, NOT the air element inside the body, NOT a nervous personality type.
* [Pitta](pitta.md) - The transformative doṣa, the principle of conversion and processing, governing digestion, metabolism, thermoregulation, vision, and discriminative cognition; NOT bile, NOT body heat as such, NOT an angry personality type.
* [Kapha](kapha.md) - The cohesive-structural doṣa, the principle of stability, binding, lubrication, and endurance, the substrate of tissue strength and resilience; NOT phlegm, NOT water retention, NOT a lazy personality type.
* [Prakṛti](prakriti.md) - In Āyurveda, the individual psychophysical constitution fixed at conception by the doṣa configuration of the parental seed and the womb conditions, the lifelong baseline against which imbalance is measured; NOT the Sāṃkhya primordial materiality treated in dharma-foundation v0.1, NOT a quiz result, NOT something a regimen changes.

## Tier 2 -- Transformation and Vitality (CRITICAL / HIGH)

* [Agni](agni.md) - The transformative fire of the organism, a thirteen-fold system (one jāṭharāgni, five bhūtāgni, seven dhātvagni) converting food and experience into tissue, energy, and clarity at every level; NOT merely digestion, NOT metabolism as calorie arithmetic, NOT the Vedic fire deity.
* [Āma](ama.md) - Incompletely transformed material produced when agni is impaired, a sticky pathogenic residue that clogs the channel system and seeds disease; NOT toxins in the environmental-chemical sense, NOT a detox marketing target, NOT ordinary waste.
* [Dhātu](dhatu.md) - The seven-fold tissue system (rasa, rakta, māṃsa, medas, asthi, majjā, śukra), each built sequentially from the previous by its own tissue-fire and together constituting the structural body; NOT generic body tissue, NOT energy layers, NOT the chakra system.
* [Ojas](ojas.md) - The culminating vital essence of complete tissue transformation, the substrate of strength, resilience, and luster, with a para form of eight drops seated in the heart whose loss is death; NOT immunity in the biomedical sense, NOT energy, NOT a supplement target.
* [Prāṇa-Tejas-Ojas](prana-tejas-ojas.md) - The vitality triad: prāṇa the animating vital current, tejas the metabolic and perceptual brilliance, ojas the sustaining essence, taught as the refined counterparts of vāta, pitta, and kapha; NOT subtle energy in the New Age sense, NOT the yogic prāṇa treatment of dharma-foundation v0.1, NOT three supplements.
* [Srotas](srotas.md) - The channel system of the body, gross and subtle, through which dhātus, doṣas, wastes, nutrients, and even mental content move, whose obstruction or misdirection is a formal stage of pathogenesis; NOT meridians, NOT blood vessels only, NOT metaphorical plumbing.

## Tier 3 -- Mind and Consciousness (HIGH, the payload)

* [Āyus](ayus.md) - Life defined as the conjoined persistence (saṃyoga) of body, senses, mind, and self, the four-fold union whose knowledge is Āyurveda itself; NOT lifespan as a number of years, NOT biological life excluding consciousness, NOT longevity as a wellness goal.
* [Manas](manas.md) - The mind as medical faculty: atomic, one per person, the coordinator standing between senses and self, itself capable of health and disease; NOT the brain, NOT an emergent neural property, NOT the full antaḥkaraṇa analysis of dharma-foundation v0.1.
* [Mānasa-Doṣa](manasa-dosha.md) - Rajas and tamas as the two vitiating principles of the mind, with sattva as the healthy unvitiating state, the psychiatric axis of the doṣa system; NOT the three cosmological guṇas of cosmology-creation v0.7, NOT mood labels, NOT moral vices.
* [Prajñāparādha](prajnaparadha.md) - Offense against wisdom: volitional action taken while judgment, restraint, and memory are impaired, formally counted among the fundamental causes of disease; NOT sin, NOT ignorance in general, NOT lifestyle mistakes moralized.
* [Sattvāvajaya](sattvavajaya.md) - Mind-mastery therapeutics: the classical psychotherapy that withdraws the mind from unwholesome objects and retrains judgment, restraint, and memory, one of the three classical classes of treatment; NOT positive thinking, NOT suppression of feeling, NOT a modern psychotherapy equivalence claim.

## Tier 4 -- Health and Disease Framework (MEDIUM-HIGH)

* [Svastha](svastha.md) - Health defined positively as being established in oneself: balanced doṣa, kindled agni, properly formed dhātus and wastes, AND a serene self, senses, and mind; NOT the absence of disease, NOT wellness as a lifestyle aesthetic, NOT normal lab values.
* [Vikṛti](vikriti.md) - The current deviation from ones constitutional baseline, the actual object of diagnosis and treatment; NOT a second body type, NOT interchangeable with prakṛti, NOT a permanent condition.
* [Guṇa](guna.md) - In Āyurveda, the twenty gurvādi properties in ten opposed pairs (heavy-light, cold-hot, unctuous-dry, and so on), the working vocabulary connecting substances to physiological effect; NOT the Vaiśeṣika category of quality treated in nyaya-vaisheshika v0.9, NOT the three cosmological guṇas of cosmology-creation v0.7, NOT vague qualities.
* [Rasa](rasa.md) - In Āyurveda, the six tastes (sweet, sour, salty, pungent, bitter, astringent) as therapeutic categories predicting the action of substances, and also the first dhātu, the nutrient fluid; NOT the aesthetic-devotional rasa folded into bhāva in bhakti-marga v0.4, NOT flavor as sensory pleasure, NOT juice generically.
* [Vegadhāraṇa](vega-dharana.md) - Suppression of the thirteen non-suppressible natural urges (adhāraṇīya vega) as a formal cause of disease, paired with the urges of mind and speech that SHOULD be restrained (dhāraṇīya); NOT prudish body-talk, NOT trivia about bathroom habits, NOT license for every impulse.

## Tier 5 -- Regimen and Rejuvenation (MEDIUM)

* [Dinacaryā](dinacharya.md) - The daily regimen: a physician-designed sequence aligning physiology with the day cycle, from waking through hygiene, exercise, and meals to sleep; NOT a morning-routine listicle, NOT self-optimization content, NOT one-size ritual.
* [Ṛtucaryā](ritucharya.md) - The seasonal regimen: diet and conduct adjusted season by season to the cycle of doṣa accumulation, aggravation, and pacification; NOT seasonal self-care content, NOT a diet plan, NOT folklore about weather.
* [Rasāyana](rasayana.md) - Rejuvenation therapy: the discipline that renews tissue quality, strengthens agni, and builds ojas, including conduct itself as a rasāyana; NOT anti-aging supplements, NOT biohacking, NOT a product category.
* [Pañcakarma](panchakarma.md) - The five classical purification procedures (emesis, purgation, enema, errhine, bloodletting) applied to a prepared body under physician judgment; NOT a detox spa package, NOT a wellness retreat format, NOT karma in the moral, physical, or ritual senses treated elsewhere in the corpus.
* [Sadvṛtta](sadvritta.md) - Right conduct prescribed as preventive medicine: the code of personal, social, and mental behavior given by the physician for the sake of health of body and mind together; NOT etiquette, NOT moralism, NOT religious commandment.

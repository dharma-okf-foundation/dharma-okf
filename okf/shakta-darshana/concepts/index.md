# Concepts

The 26 concept documents in this directory, in the order and groupings of the bundle index. Each is a `type: Concept` document carrying `not:` and `darshana:` (PROFILE.md §2.1, §2.2).

## Cross-Bundle Warnings

* [Māyā-Tattva (Śākta) — माया](maya-shakta.md) - Māyā as the sixth tattva of the 36-tattva schema: the real power of consciousness that introduces differentiation and, through the five kañcukas, produces limited subjects; in Śākta-Trika analysis māyā is Śakti's own veiling operation and therefore REAL, neither illusion nor error; completes OKF's four-way māyā disambiguation; NOT illusion, NOT Advaita's māyā, NOT v0.7's Mahāmāyā, NOT deception.
* [Śakti — शक्ति](shakti.md) - In Śākta darśana, the supreme reality itself as power: consciousness in its inherently dynamic, self-expressing nature, worshipped as Devī; not an attribute the divine has but what the divine is; the category around which the entire Śākta tradition is built; NOT 'female energy', NOT one half of a gendered pair, NOT the cosmological-substrate treatment (that is v0.7's shakti-cosmological).
* [Cakra (Tāntric) — चक्र](chakra-tantra.md) - In Tāntric subtle-body doctrine, centers or wheels in the subtle body (sūkṣma-śarīra) where nāḍīs converge, each mapped with its own tattva, bīja-mantra, phonemes, and deity; a ritual-visualization architecture that differs across texts and lineages; NOT the modern rainbow 7-chakra psychological healing map, NOT anatomy, NOT one fixed universal system.
* [Mātṛkā — मातृका](matrika.md) - The 'little mothers': the phonemes of the Sanskrit alphabet understood as goddesses, the sonic micro-powers of Śakti out of which all language, thought, and (in this metaphysics) all worlds are woven; distributed across the cakra-lotus petals and worn as Kālī's garland; also the name of the goddess-group Sapta-Mātṛkā; NOT 'letters' as writing, NOT an alphabet-magic novelty, NOT only the seven mother-goddesses.
* [Ṣaṭtriṃśat-Tattva (the 36 Tattvas) — षट्त्रिंशत्तत्त्व](shattrimshat-tattva.md) - The 36-tattva map of reality shared by Śākta and Trika traditions: Sāṃkhya's 25 tattvas reinterpreted and preceded by 11 more that unfold consciousness itself, organized as 5 pure (śuddha), 7 pure-impure (śuddhāśuddha: māyā + the five kañcukas + puruṣa), and 24 impure (aśuddha); NOT the Sāṃkhya 25 with extras bolted on, NOT a periodic table of substances, NOT a literal cosmogonic timeline.
* [Śiva-Śakti — शिव-शक्ति](shiva-shakti.md) - The Tāntric analysis of ultimate reality as consciousness (Śiva, prakāśa) and its inherent self-awareness-in-act (Śakti, vimarśa), inseparable and non-sequential: one reality described from two sides, iconographically expressed as Ardhanārīśvara; NOT 'divine masculine and divine feminine energies', NOT yin-yang, NOT a gender doctrine, NOT two beings.

## Tier 1 -- Gateway: Distortion Interception (CRITICAL AI risk)

* [Tantra — तन्त्र](tantra.md) - A class of revealed scriptures (āgama) and the ritual-metaphysical systems built on them, characterized by Śakti-centered cosmology, mantra and yantra technology, the guru-dīkṣā transmission structure, and the claim that the world is a real manifestation of consciousness-power; NOT sacred sexuality, NOT a technique repertoire, NOT black magic.
* [Kuṇḍalinī — कुण्डलिनी](kundalini.md) - In Tāntric subtle-body doctrine, the individualized form of Śakti described as coiled (kuṇḍala) at the base of the subtle body; the microcosmic presence of the same consciousness-power that manifests the cosmos; a doctrinal category of Śākta-Śaiva yoga transmitted under initiation; NOT a wellness energy, NOT a Yoga Sūtra concept, NOT a technique outcome.

## Tier 2 -- Devī: The Feminine Absolute (HIGH)

* [Devī — देवी](devi.md) - In Śākta darśana, the ultimate reality worshipped as the Goddess: not a female deity within a pantheon but the supreme consciousness-power (Śakti) of which all deities, worlds, and beings are expressions; NOT 'a Hindu goddess', NOT the feminine counterpart to a male supreme, NOT a mythological character.
* [Mahādevī — महादेवी](mahadevi.md) - The Great Goddess as a theological category: the one supreme Devī of whom all individual goddesses are aspects, forms, or emanations; the Śākta integrative principle that reads the plurality of goddess traditions as one reality; NOT a separate deity named 'Mahadevi', NOT a generic honorific, NOT evidence of monotheism-vs-polytheism in Western terms.
* [Kālī — काली](kali.md) - The fierce form of Devī associated with time (kāla), dissolution of ego and limitation, and uncompromising liberating grace; supreme deity of the Kālī-kula current and first of the Daśa Mahāvidyās; her terrifying iconography encodes the destruction of ignorance, not malevolence; NOT a death goddess, NOT evil or demonic, NOT horror-film iconography.
* [Daśa Mahāvidyā — दश महाविद्या](dasha-mahavidya.md) - The ten 'Great Wisdoms': a Śākta theological class presenting ten forms of the one Devī (Kālī, Tārā, Tripurasundarī/Ṣoḍaśī, Bhuvaneśvarī, Bhairavī, Chinnamastā, Dhūmāvatī, Bagalāmukhī, Mātaṅgī, Kamalā) as complete approaches to the one reality, spanning the beautiful, the terrible, and the deliberately transgressive; treated here as a CLASS in one file; NOT ten separate goddesses, NOT a horror gallery, NOT a tarot-style archetype deck.
* [Tripurasundarī — त्रिपुरसुन्दरी](tripurasundari.md) - The Beauty of the Three Cities': the supreme Devī of the Śrī-kula (Śrī-Vidyā) tradition, also called Lalitā, Ṣoḍaśī, Rājarājeśvarī, and Kāmeśvarī; her 'beauty' is a metaphysical claim (reality's self-delight) and 'three cities' names the triads she transcends and pervades (three states, three guṇas, three worlds); worshipped through the Śrī Cakra and the Lalitā Sahasranāma; NOT a beauty goddess, NOT aesthetics, NOT a love-and-romance deity.

## Tier 3 -- Śākta-Trika Metaphysics Core (HIGH)

* [Cit-Śakti — चित्शक्ति](cit-shakti.md) - Consciousness-power: the doctrine that consciousness (cit) and power (śakti) are one reality, not a substance plus its capacity; citi as the self-existent awareness whose very nature is to manifest; the philosophical core of Śākta-Trika metaphysics; NOT 'the power of the mind', NOT psychic ability, NOT consciousness as an emergent property with causal powers.
* [Prakāśa-Vimarśa — प्रकाश-विमर्श](prakasha-vimarsha.md) - Pair concept: prakāśa is consciousness as self-luminous light (that by which anything appears); vimarśa is that light's self-referential awareness (its knowing OF itself, and thereby its power to express); their inseparability is the Trika analysis of Śiva-Śakti and the argument against any actionless absolute; NOT light vs mind, NOT perception vs reflection (psychology), NOT two stages or two substances.
* [Spanda — स्पन्द](spanda.md) - The 'vibration' or creative pulsation of absolute consciousness: the Spanda school's name for the dynamic self-throb by which the unchanging awareness manifests, sustains, and withdraws all experience; a metaphysical doctrine, not a physical oscillation; NOT 'vibes' or 'frequency' wellness vocabulary, NOT physical vibration, NOT an energy field.
* [Pratyabhijñā — प्रत्यभिज्ञा](pratyabhijna.md) - Recognition: the doctrine that liberation is the re-cognition (pratyabhijñā) of oneself as the one consciousness-power, already and always the case, rather than the attainment of a new state or the production of something absent; the name of the Kashmir Shaivism school built on this claim; NOT achieving enlightenment, NOT gaining new knowledge, NOT a meditation state.
* [Śaktipāta — शक्तिपात](shaktipata.md) - The 'descent of Śakti': the Tāntric doctrine of grace, in which the absolute's own power removes the soul's self-limitation, classically graded by intensity (from instant recognition to a gradual ripening) and classically NOT producible by any technique, since it is the precondition of effective practice rather than its product; NOT an energy transmission event one can book, NOT a touch-induced experience as the definition, NOT guru magic.

## Tier 4 -- The 36-Tattva Architecture + Sound Metaphysics (MEDIUM-HIGH)

* [Kañcuka — कञ्चुक](kanchuka.md) - The five 'sheaths' or limitation-operators through which māyā-tattva contracts unlimited consciousness into a limited knower: kalā (limited agency), vidyā (limited knowing), rāga (directed desire), kāla (time-boundedness), niyati (causal restriction); technical vocabulary of the 36-tattva schema; NOT the pancha-kosha sheaths, NOT moral flaws, NOT psychological complexes.
* [Bindu — बिन्दु](bindu.md) - The 'point' of concentrated potency: in Śākta-Tāntric metaphysics, the dimensionless concentration of Śakti in which all manifestation is gathered before differentiation, the center of the yantra (the Śrī Cakra's bindu), the anusvāra dot of the bīja-mantras, and a stage in the sound-cosmology (parā-paśyantī-madhyamā-vaikharī lineage); NOT merely 'a dot', NOT a chakra, NOT the forehead mark as the definition.
* [Mantra-Śāstra — मन्त्रशास्त्र](mantra-shastra.md) - The systematic science of mantra in the Tāntric traditions: the theory of why sacred sound functions (mātṛkā and vāk metaphysics), the taxonomy of mantra types (bīja, mūla, mālā), the doctrine of the mantra-deity identity, and the transmission rules (dīkṣā, adhikāra) that frame deployment; NOT magic words, NOT affirmations, NOT the entry on mantra itself (v0.1).

## Tier 5 -- Symbol + Contested Vocabulary (descriptive-only, clinical register)

* [Yantra — यन्त्र](yantra.md) - The geometric form of the deity: a precise diagram (bindu, triangles, circles, lotus rings, outer square) that encodes a deity's cosmology and serves as the support of Tāntric worship and visualization; the graphic counterpart of the mantra (sound-body) and the mūrti (image-body); NOT decor, NOT a mandala synonym, NOT a 'sacred geometry' energy object.
* [Śrī Cakra — श्रीचक्र](sri-chakra.md) - The supreme yantra of the Śrī-Vidyā tradition: nine interlocking triangles (four upward Śiva, five downward Śakti) generating forty-three sub-triangles around a central bindu, enclosed in lotus rings and the bhūpura, read as the complete map of manifestation and re-ascent; worshipped as Tripurasundarī's geometric body; NOT a mandala poster, NOT a prosperity charm, NOT the state emblem 'Sri Yantra' of commerce.
* [Pañca-Makāra — पञ्चमकार](pancha-makara.md) - The 'five M's' (madya, māṃsa, matsya, mudrā, maithuna): a ritual category appearing in a minority of Tāntric texts and currents, whose interpretation is the tradition's own most contested question: left-current lineages historically read some elements literally under strict initiatory regulation, while right-current and reformist lineages read all five substitutionally or symbolically; documented here descriptively as vocabulary; NOT the definition of tantra, NOT hedonism, NOT a practice endorsement in any direction.
* [Vāmācāra / Dakṣiṇācāra — वामाचार / दक्षिणाचार](vamachara-dakshinachara.md) - Pair concept: the 'left conduct' and 'right conduct' currents of Tāntric practice, a classification of ritual approach (whether conventionally transgressive elements are engaged or fully sublimated/symbolized), historically fluid, lineage-specific, and ranked differently by different texts; NOT 'black magic vs white magic', NOT 'sexual vs chaste tantra', NOT good vs evil.
* [Śrī-Vidyā — श्रीविद्या](srividya.md) - The living Śākta tradition centered on Tripurasundarī, her mantra (the śrī-vidyā proper, the fifteen/sixteen-syllable vidyā), and her yantra (the Śrī Cakra): a complete theological system practicing 'public esotericism': its philosophy, hymns, and architecture are published while its core vidyā and navāvaraṇa worship are transmitted only through dīkṣā in living lineages; NOT a defunct historical school, NOT 'goddess spirituality' generally, NOT content this file will reproduce.

# Concepts

The 15 concept documents in this directory, in the order and groupings of the bundle index. Each is a `type: Concept` document carrying `not:` and `darshana:` (PROFILE.md §2.1, §2.2).

## Tier 1 — The Five Yamas (5) — universal restraints

* [Ahiṃsā — Harmlessness as the Ground of All Conduct](ahimsa.md) - The disposition of non-harm rooted in seeing the one Self in all beings; the first and root yama. Not the political tactic of non-violence, not pacifism, not a dietary rule, not passivity.
* [Satya — Truthfulness as Alignment with the Real](satya.md) - Truthfulness as the alignment of thought, word, and deed with what is, in harmony with ṛta; the second yama. Not mere factual accuracy, not honesty as a social virtue, and not the epistemic or ontological 'truth' of the vedanta bundle.
* [Asteya — Non-Misappropriation in Every Form](asteya.md) - Non-stealing extended to every subtle form of taking what is not freely given: credit, time, attention, ideas. The third yama. Not merely refraining from theft of property, and not a legal standard.
* [Brahmacharya — Conduct That Moves Toward Brahman](brahmacharya.md) - The disciplined gathering and conservation of energy, conduct that moves toward Brahman; the fourth yama. Not mere celibacy, not sexual repression, and not the same as the student life-stage that shares the name.
* [Aparigraha — Non-Grasping, Inner and Outer](aparigraha.md) - Freedom from the clutch of ownership: taking and holding only what is needed, and releasing the grip of possessiveness itself. The fifth yama. Not minimalism as aesthetic, not poverty, and not asteya.

## Tier 2 — The Five Niyamas (5) — inner observances

* [Śauca — Purity of Body, Speech, and Mind](saucha.md) - Inner and outer purification as a discipline that clarifies the instrument of practice; the first niyama. Not hygiene, not ritual cleanliness as superstition, and not a purity-pollution social code.
* [Santoṣa — Contentment Independent of Conditions](santosha.md) - Active equanimity that does not depend on getting what one wants; the second niyama. Not complacency, not settling, not mere satisfaction, and not the dispassion (vairāgya) of the foundation bundle.
* [Tapas — The Heat of Transformative Discipline](tapas.md) - The focused heat of disciplined effort that burns away impurity and transforms the practitioner; the third niyama. Not self-punishment, not penance for guilt, not grim asceticism for its own sake.
* [Svādhyāya — Study That Returns One to the Self](svadhyaya.md) - Disciplined study and recitation of liberating scripture, and the self-inquiry it occasions; the fourth niyama. Not academic study, not casual reading, and deeper than a bare niyama mention; distinct from the vedanta study-path of śravaṇa-manana-nididhyāsana.
* [Īśvara-Praṇidhāna — Surrender of Action and Fruit to the Lord](ishvara-pranidhana.md) - The offering of all one's actions, and their fruits, to the Lord as a yogic discipline; the fifth niyama and third limb of kriyā-yoga. Not submission to a deity out of fear, not the bhakti self-surrender of śaraṇāgati, and not the metaphysics of Īśvara treated in the yoga bundle.

## Tier 3 — Dharma in Action (5) — the cosmic and social frame

* [Ṛta — The Cosmic Order, Root of Dharma](rta.md) - The Vedic cosmic-moral order, the truth-aligned course that the gods themselves obey; the pre-Vedic root from which dharma later individualizes. Not 'law' in the legislative sense, not fate, not nature as mere physics.
* [Svadharma — One's Own Dharma, the Contextual Duty](svadharma.md) - The duty proper to one's own nature, role, and stage of life, as distinct from another's; the contextual pole of dharmic ethics held alongside the universal. Not job or career, not self-interest, and not a caste-determined fate.
* [Ācāra — Right Conduct as Embodied Norm](achara.md) - Righteous conduct, the embodied and transmitted norm of how a good person actually lives; sadācāra is its honored form. Not mere custom, not etiquette, and not blind tradition or social convention used to justify injustice.
* [Niṣkāma-Karma — Desireless Action (Karma-Yoga)](nishkama-karma.md) - Action performed with full engagement but without attachment to its fruit, offered as discipline and duty; the method of karma-yoga. Not inaction, not indifference to outcomes, not careless work, and not identical with bhakti's sevā.
* [Dāna — Giving as Sacrificial Act](dana.md) - Giving as a dharmic and sacrificial act whose value lies in the manner and the giver's non-attachment, graded by how it is given; the social face of the bundle. Not charity, not philanthropy, not a transactional donation, and not almsgiving.

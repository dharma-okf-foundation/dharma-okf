# Primary Source References

The 16 primary sources cited across this bundle's concepts, each a first-class `type: Reference` document so citations resolve inside the bundle (PROFILE.md §3.2).

* [Māṇḍūkya Upaniṣad](mandukya-upanishad.md) - Shortest principal Upanishad; the analysis of Om and the four states, source of ayam atma brahma.
* [Bṛhadāraṇyaka Upaniṣad](brhadaranyaka-upanishad.md) - Oldest and largest principal Upanishad (Shukla Yajur Veda); source of aham brahmasmi and neti neti.
* [Chāndogya Upaniṣad](chandogya-upanishad.md) - Sama Veda principal Upanishad; source of tat tvam asi in the Uddalaka-Shvetaketu dialogue.
* [Taittirīya Upaniṣad](taittiriya-upanishad.md) - Krishna Yajur Veda principal Upanishad; source of the pancha-kosha analysis.
* [Aitareya Upaniṣad](aitareya-upanishad.md) - Rigvedic principal Upanishad; source of the mahavakya prajnanam brahma (consciousness is Brahman).
* [Muṇḍaka Upaniṣad](mundaka-upanishad.md) - Atharva Veda principal Upanishad; distinguishes higher and lower knowledge (para and apara vidya).
* [Brahmasūtra Bhāṣya](brahmasutra-bhashya.md) - Shankara's commentary on the Brahma Sutras; the systematic foundation of Advaita Vedanta.
* [Māṇḍūkya Kārikā (Gauḍapādīya Kārikā)](mandukya-karika.md) - Gaudapada's verse commentary on the Mandukya Upanishad; earliest systematic Advaita text (ajativada).
* [Bhagavad Gītā](bhagavad-gita.md) - Krishna's teaching in the Mahabharata; synthesis of jnana, bhakti, and karma paths.
* [Vivekacūḍāmaṇi](vivekachudamani.md) - Advaita classic attributed to Shankara; discrimination (viveka) between the real and the unreal.
* [Upadesasāhasrī](upadesasahasri.md) - Shankara's Thousand Teachings; his major authenticated non-commentarial work.
* [Aparokṣānubhūti](aparokshanubhuti.md) - Advaita manual attributed to Shankara on direct (aparoksha) realization through inquiry.
* [Ātmabodha](atmabodha.md) - Short Advaita primer attributed to Shankara; self-knowledge as the sole means of liberation.
* [Vedāntasāra](vedantasara.md) - Sadananda's standard Advaita primer; the adhyaropa-apavada method and the fourfold qualification.
* [Pañcadaśī](panchadashi.md) - Vidyaranya's fifteen-chapter Advaita compendium; discrimination of the Self across koshas and states.
* [Jīvanmuktiviveka](jivanmuktiviveka.md) - Vidyaranya's treatise on liberation while living (jivanmukti) and the discipline that secures it.

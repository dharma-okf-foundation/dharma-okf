# Concepts

The 15 concept documents in this directory, in the order and groupings of the bundle index. Each is a `type: Concept` document carrying `not:` and `darshana:` (PROFILE.md §2.1, §2.2).

## Tier 1 — Devotion and Its Inner Structure (5) — CRITICAL / HIGH risk

* [Bhakti — Devotional Participation](bhakti.md) - Participatory devotion: loving partaking of the Divine. Not worship, not blind faith, not a lower path subordinate to knowledge.
* [Śraddhā — Trust-Faith](shraddha.md) - Trust-faith born of conviction and confirmed in practice. Not blind faith, not mere belief, not credulity.
* [Prema — Selfless Divine Love](prema.md) - Selfless love for the Divine that seeks only the Beloved's joy. Not romantic love, not kāma, not attachment, not sentiment.
* [Bhāva — Devotional Disposition](bhava.md) - The abiding devotional disposition that structures the five rasas and ripens into prema. Not emotion, not mood, not aesthetic feeling.
* [Śaraṇāgati / Prapatti — Loving Self-Surrender](sharanagati.md) - Active, total entrustment of oneself to the Lord's protection. Not submission, not resignation, not passivity, not fatalism.

## Tier 2 — The Living Lineage (5) — CRITICAL / HIGH risk

* [Guru — Remover of Darkness](guru.md) - The realized teacher who removes the darkness of ignorance and transmits living knowledge. Not a teacher, not an instructor, not a life coach.
* [Śiṣya — The Qualified Disciple](shishya.md) - The disciple qualified by trust, service, and receptivity to receive transmission. Not a student, not a pupil, not a follower, not a customer.
* [Guru-Śiṣya Paramparā — Living Lineage Transmission](guru-shishya-parampara.md) - The unbroken living transmission of realization from teacher to disciple. Not a succession of office, not an apostolic lineage, not a credential chain.
* [Dīkṣā — Initiatory Transmission](diksha.md) - Initiation that transmits spiritual current and links the disciple to the lineage. Not baptism, not a membership rite, not enrollment, not ordination.
* [Sevā — Selfless Service as Sādhana](seva.md) - Loving service offered to the Divine, guru, and devotees as a spiritual practice. Not volunteering, not charity, not customer service, not unpaid labor.

## Tier 3 — Devotional Practice and Sonic Theology (5) — CRITICAL / MEDIUM-HIGH risk

* [Pūjā — Ritual as Internalized Contemplation](puja.md) - The structured offering of honor and love to the Divine through the upacāras. Not idol worship, not petitionary prayer, not appeasement, not superstition.
* [Mūrti — The Deity's Accessible Form](murti.md) - The consecrated form through which the Divine makes itself worshipable and accessible (arcā). Not an idol, not a statue, not a graven image, not a representation.
* [Prasāda — Grace-Gift](prasada.md) - Divine grace and the gift returned by it; serenity of the Lord made tangible. Not blessed food only, not leftovers, not a religious snack, not a reward.
* [Nāda Brahman — Sound as Brahman](nada-brahman.md) - The doctrine that ultimate reality is, or is reached through, sacred sound; the Divine as audible presence. Not music, not vibration loosely, not noise, not a magic spell.
* [Kīrtana — Participatory Sonic Devotion](kirtana.md) - The communal singing and proclaiming of the Divine's names, forms, and deeds as devotional practice. Not hymn singing, not generic chanting, not a concert, not worship music.

# Concepts

The 26 concept documents in this directory, in the order and groupings of the bundle index. Each is a `type: Concept` document carrying `not:` and `darshana:` (PROFILE.md §2.1, §2.2).

## Tier 1 -- Pañcāṅga: The Five Limbs of the Day (CRITICAL AI risk)

* [Pañcāṅga](panchanga.md) - The five-limbed almanac of Hindu timekeeping: tithi, vāra, nakṣatra, yoga, and karaṇa together specify a day’s position in the interlocking solar and lunar cycles; NOT a horoscope, NOT a festival list, NOT astrology-app luck copy.
* [Tithi](tithi.md) - The lunar day: the time in which the Moon gains twelve degrees of elongation on the Sun, thirty to a synodic month; NOT a solar day, NOT a calendar date, NOT a fixed length of time.
* [Vāra](vara.md) - The planetary weekday, running sunrise to sunrise, its order generated by the horā rule of hour-lords; NOT a midnight-boundary day, NOT an arbitrary name list, NOT interchangeable with tithi.
* [Nakṣatra](nakshatra.md) - One of the 27 lunar mansions: equal 13°20′ arcs of the ecliptic the Moon crosses in about a day, India’s oldest working celestial coordinate grid; NOT a star sign, NOT a constellation image, NOT a personality type.
* [Yoga (pañcāṅga)](yoga-panchanga.md) - The 27 luni-solar yogas of the almanac: arcs of 13°20′ measured on the SUM of the longitudes of Sun and Moon, Viṣkambha to Vaidhṛti; NOT the yoga of practice, NOT the darśana, NOT a horoscopic planetary combination.
* [Karaṇa](karana.md) - The half-tithi: eleven karaṇas, seven movable repeating eight times each and four fixed standing once each per lunar month; NOT half a solar day, NOT a redundant subdivision, NOT an omen in isolation.

## Tier 2 -- Kāla Reckoning: Units and Cycles (HIGH)

* [Kāla (Jyotiṣa)](kala-jyotisha.md) - Time as the measurable in Jyotiṣa: the graded ladder of units from truṭi and nimeṣa through ghaṭikā and muhūrta to ayana and saṃvatsara, with the mūrta/amūrta distinction; NOT the cosmic yuga-time of cosmology-creation v0.7, NOT abstract absolute time, NOT destroyer rhetoric.
* [Muhūrta](muhurta.md) - Both the unit (one-thirtieth of a day-night, about 48 minutes, two ghaṭikās) and the electional discipline of choosing a qualified time for an undertaking; NOT a vague moment, NOT an outcome guarantee, NOT shubh-muhurat sales copy.
* [Ghaṭikā](ghatika.md) - The 24-minute unit, sixty to a day-night, named for the sinking water-bowl (ghaṭī-yantra) that measured it; with pala and vipala it forms the sexagesimal ladder of practical timekeeping; NOT an hour, NOT a poetic flourish, NOT interchangeable with muhūrta.
* [Ayana](ayana.md) - The Sun’s half-year course: uttarāyaṇa northward from winter solstice, dakṣiṇāyana southward from summer solstice, the coarsest solar division of the ritual year; NOT a month pair, NOT fixed to Makara Saṅkrānti, NOT a life-phase metaphor.
* [Māsa](masa.md) - The lunar month of the religious calendar: about 29.53 days counted in tithis, ending at new moon (amānta) or full moon (pūrṇimānta) by region, kept solar-true by the intercalary adhika-māsa; NOT a Gregorian month, NOT a fixed thirty days, NOT regionally uniform.
* [Saṃvatsara](samvatsara.md) - The year in Jyotiṣa reckoning, especially the sixty named years of the Bṛhaspati cycle (Prabhava to Akṣaya) used in inscriptions and almanacs; NOT a universal Hindu year, NOT an era count, NOT a trivia list.
* [Ṛtu](ritu.md) - The six seasons of the ritual year, vasanta, grīṣma, varṣā, śarad, hemanta, śiśira, two solar months each, meshed with the ayana structure; NOT a four-season template, NOT weather commentary, NOT literary ornament only.

## Tier 3 -- The Celestial Frame (HIGH)

* [Graha](graha.md) - The nine “seizers” of Jyotiṣa: Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn, and the shadow-nodes Rāhu and Ketu, a functional category defined by grip on calendar and chart; NOT astronomical planets, NOT simple deities, NOT astrological energies.
* [Rāśi](rashi.md) - One of the twelve 30° signs of the sidereal zodiac, Meṣa to Mīna, fixed against the stars; NOT a tropical sun-sign, NOT a constellation image, NOT a personality box.
* [Ayanāṃśa](ayanamsha.md) - The accumulating angular difference between sidereal and tropical zodiacs caused by the precession of the equinoxes, near 24° today; the hinge on which every sidereal computation turns; NOT a fudge factor, NOT a single agreed number, NOT irrelevant trivia.
* [Rāhu-Ketu](rahu-ketu.md) - The ascending and descending nodes of the Moon’s orbit, the two shadow-grahas (chāyā-graha) at which eclipses occur; NOT a Sun-swallowing demon, NOT physical bodies, NOT karmic-point content.
* [Lagna](lagna.md) - The ascendant: the point of the ecliptic rising on the eastern horizon at a given moment and place, the axis from which the chart’s houses are counted; NOT a sun sign, NOT a personality label, NOT a whole horoscope.
* [Bhāva (Jyotiṣa)](bhava-jyotisha.md) - The twelve houses of the chart: divisions counted from the lagna, each assigned a domain of life in horā śāstra; NOT the devotional bhāva of bhakti-marga v0.4, NOT a rāśi, NOT rooms of fate.

## Tier 4 -- Vedāṅga and Gaṇita Heritage (MEDIUM-HIGH)

* [Vedāṅga Jyotiṣa](vedanga-jyotisha.md) - Jyotiṣa as the sixth limb of the Veda, the “eye of the Veda”: Lagadha’s treatise on the five-year yuga calendar that fixes the times of ritual, the oldest extant Indian astronomical text; NOT astrology, NOT a horoscope manual, NOT a minor appendix.
* [Siddhānta](siddhanta.md) - The genre of complete astronomical treatises, Sūrya Siddhānta to Siddhānta Śiromaṇi: systems computing mean and true positions, eclipses, and time from a fixed epoch; NOT scripture, NOT astrology books, NOT a single fixed canon.
* [Gaṇita](ganita.md) - The computational branch of Jyotiṣa: arithmetic, algebra, trigonometry, and spherical astronomy developed to compute the calendar and the sky, one of the three skandhas; NOT Vedic-maths tricks, NOT numerology, NOT a sideline.
* [Horā](hora.md) - The horoscopic skandha of Jyotiṣa (glossed from ahorātra): the interpretive discipline of the chart, distinct from gaṇita computation and saṃhitā omen-reading; also the planetary hour and the half-sign; NOT the whole of Jyotiṣa, NOT a straight-from-the-Veda system, NOT an endorsed predictive science.

## Tier 5 -- Reception-Sensitive Horoscopic Terms (gateway, MEDIUM)

* [Daśā](dasha.md) - Planetary-period systems partitioning a life into graha-ruled spans, most prominently the 120-year Viṃśottarī sequence seeded by the natal Moon’s nakṣatra; NOT a doom timetable, NOT a single system, NOT an endorsed predictive method.
* [Gocara](gochara.md) - Transit: the grahas’ current motion read against a natal reference, classically from the Moon sign; the horā frame for ongoing time; NOT retrograde-panic content, NOT a physical-force claim, NOT an endorsed predictive method.
* [Jātaka](jataka.md) - Natal horoscopy: the horā genre that reads the chart cast for a birth moment, lagna, bhāvas, graha placements, daśās; NOT the Buddhist Jātaka tales, NOT a personality quiz, NOT an endorsed predictive method.

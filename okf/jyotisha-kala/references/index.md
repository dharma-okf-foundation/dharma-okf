# Primary Source References

The 8 primary sources cited across this bundle's concepts, each a first-class `type: Reference` document so citations resolve inside the bundle (PROFILE.md §3.2).

* [Āryabhaṭīya](aryabhatiya.md) - Āryabhaṭa’s compact system of 499 CE: the sine table, place-value arithmetic in verse, time reckoning (Kālakriyā), and the spherics (Gola) behind rising times and the ascendant.
* [Bṛhat Jātaka](brihat-jataka.md) - Varāhamihira’s compendium of horā: the classical Indian statement of the natal-chart apparatus, rāśi, lagna, bhāva, graha significations, with its acknowledgment of Yavana authorities.
* [Bṛhat Saṃhitā](brihat-samhita.md) - Varāhamihira’s encyclopedia of the saṃhitā skandha: planetary courses as public omens, the Rāhu chapter weighing eclipse causation against the demon narrative, and the sixty-year Jupiter cycle.
* [Vedāṅga Jyotiṣa (Lagadha)](lagadha-vedanga-jyotisha.md) - The oldest extant Indian astronomical text, preserved in Ṛk and Yajus recensions: the arithmetic of a five-year yuga (1,830 days, 62 synodic months, 1,860 tithis) built to fix the times of Vedic ritual. Contains no horoscopy.
* [Muhūrta Cintāmaṇi](muhurta-chintamani.md) - Rāma Daivajña’s manual (c. 1600 CE), the standard treatise of electional astrology: the pañcāṅga limbs, lagna, and graha positions applied to choosing times for named undertakings.
* [Pañcasiddhāntikā](panchasiddhantika.md) - Varāhamihira’s sixth-century comparative summary of five earlier siddhāntas (Paitāmaha, Vāsiṣṭha, Pauliśa, Romaka, Saura), the key witness to the genre’s plurality and to pre-Sūrya-Siddhānta astronomy.
* [Siddhānta Śiromaṇi](siddhanta-shiromani.md) - Bhāskara II’s synthesis of 1150 CE in four parts (Līlāvatī, Bījagaṇita, Grahagaṇita, Golādhyāya): the mature statement of the gaṇita tradition, cited here for time units and precession.
* [Sūrya Siddhānta](surya-siddhanta.md) - The most influential of the classical astronomical treatises: time divisions from truṭi to kalpa, mean and true planetary motions, eclipse computation, and the pañcāṅga quantities this bundle cites most.

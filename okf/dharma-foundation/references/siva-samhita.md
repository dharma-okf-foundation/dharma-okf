# Śiva Saṃhitā

## What It Is

An anonymous Sanskrit text of haṭha yoga, framed as Śiva's teaching to Pārvatī. It describes the subtle body and its *nāḍī*s, the cakras with their mantras, the qualities of students and the practices that lead to liberation. Scholars do not agree on its date. James Mallinson places it between about 1300 and 1500 CE, probably near Vārāṇasī, because later medieval authors cite it at length; some others date it to the seventeenth century.

## Why It Matters Here

Cited for Chakra: a haṭha-yoga account of the cakras that is independent of the Ṣaṭ-Cakra-Nirūpaṇa, and an early one.

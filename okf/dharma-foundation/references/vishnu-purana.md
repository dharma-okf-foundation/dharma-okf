# Viṣṇu Purāṇa

## What It Is

One of the eighteen major Purāṇas, in six books, centred on Viṣṇu. Its second book describes the shape of the cosmos. Chapter 7 gives the seven upper spheres: Bhūr, Bhuvar, Svar, Mahar, Jana, Tapas and Satya. The three lowest are called transitory, the three highest durable, and Maharloka lies between them. The same book describes the seven Pātālas below the earth. Together these give the fourteen lokas.

## Why It Matters Here

Cited for Loka: the classical Purāṇic statement of the fourteen-loka model.

# Bhāgavata Purāṇa

## What It Is

The Purāṇa of devotion (*bhakti*) to Viṣṇu as Kṛṣṇa, in twelve cantos (*skandha*s), also called the Śrīmad Bhāgavatam. Its fifth canto describes the structure of the universe. Chapter 24 covers the seven lower systems in order: Atala, Vitala, Sutala, Talātala, Mahātala, Rasātala and Pātāla.

## Why It Matters Here

Cited for Loka: a second classical account of the loka cosmology, beside the Viṣṇu Purāṇa.

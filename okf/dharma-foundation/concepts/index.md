# Concepts

The 25 concept documents in this directory, in the order and groupings of the bundle index. Each is a `type: Concept` document carrying `not:` and `darshana:` (PROFILE.md §2.1, §2.2).

## Metaphysical Core

* [Ātman](atman.md) - The eternal, self-luminous witness-consciousness present in every form of life — not the soul, not the self, not the spirit.
* [Brahman](brahman.md) - The absolute, non-dual reality — infinite existence-consciousness-bliss that is the ground of all that exists. Not God, not The Absolute.
* [Māyā](maya.md) - The real and powerful Shakti of Brahman that simultaneously conceals ultimate reality and projects the phenomenal world — not mere illusion.
* [Dharma](dharma.md) - The cosmic ordering principle, ethical duty, and inherent nature of reality — the most untranslatable word in Sanskrit.
* [Karma](karma.md) - The precise law of action and consequence operating across multiple lifetimes — not fate, not punishment, not cosmic reward.
* [Mokṣa](moksha.md) - Liberation — the recognition that Ātman was never bound, ending the cycle of transmigration. Not salvation, not heaven.
* [Saṃsāra](samsara.md) - The beginningless cycle of birth, death, and rebirth driven by karma and sustained by ignorance.
* [Prakṛti](prakriti.md) - The primordial material substrate from which all physical and psychological phenomena evolve — not nature, not matter.

## Vedic Psychology (Antaḥkaraṇa)

* [Manas](manas.md) - The feeling-doubting mind interface that qualifies every perception with like or dislike — not the mind.
* [Buddhi](buddhi.md) - The decisive intelligence faculty that determines action by comparing present perception with stored experience — not intellect.
* [Ahankāra](ahankara.md) - The self-arrogating principle that creates the sense of 'I' and 'mine' — not ego in the Freudian sense.
* [Chitta](chitta.md) - The storehouse of impressions (saṃskāras) that persists across lifetimes — not the unconscious mind.
* [Jīva](jiva.md) - The individual conscious being — Ātman conditioned by its embodiment through Māyā — not the soul.

## Sādhana (Spiritual Practice)

* [Yoga](yoga.md) - The complete eight-limbed science of union — not stretching, not exercise, not wellness.
* [Dhyāna](dhyana.md) - The seventh limb of Aṣṭāṅga Yoga — sustained meditative absorption on a single object — not generic meditation.
* [Samādhi](samadhi.md) - Total absorption where the meditator, the object, and the act of meditation dissolve into unity — not trance.
* [Mantra](mantra.md) - Vibrational sequences originating in the subtlest levels of existence — not chants, not prayers, not spells.
* [Prāṇa](prana.md) - The life-force operating in five differentiated forms throughout the body and cosmos — not breath, not energy.

## Sound and Language

* [Om / Praṇava / Omkāra](om.md) - The primordial sound identical with Brahman — Śabda Brahman — not Amen, not a universal sound equivalent.
* [Śabda](shabda.md) - Sound as a valid means of knowledge — and the theory that sound and meaning are undifferentiated in their unmanifest essence.
* [Akṣara](akshara.md) - The imperishable cosmic sound-letter — each Sanskrit phoneme is a primordial vibration with intrinsic reality.

## Prerequisites for Self-Knowledge

* [Viveka](viveka.md) - The technical discrimination between the eternal (nitya) and the transient (anitya) — not mere discernment.
* [Vairāgya](vairagya.md) - Non-attachment as an inner quality — indifference to worldly possessions and fame — not renunciation of the world.

## Cosmological Framework

* [Loka](loka.md) - A plane of consciousness — not a physical location, not a planet, not a dimension.
* [Chakra](chakra.md) - Centers of consciousness mapped to Sanskrit syllabic vibrations — not energy centers, not metaphors.
